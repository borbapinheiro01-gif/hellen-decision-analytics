## Selected Work

### GN–ADMM  
**Constrained Nonlinear Optimization · Algorithms · Scientific Computing**

Research and development of a hybrid numerical method integrating **Gauss–Newton and ADMM** for constrained nonlinear least-squares problems.

Main themes:

- nonlinear optimization under equality and inequality constraints;
- parameter estimation in dynamic systems;
- numerical convergence and stability;
- sensitivity analysis;
- structural identifiability;
- reproducible computational validation;
- extensive numerical experimentation.

The project reflects my core mathematical background and my transition from theoretical mathematics to computational modeling and algorithm development.

---

### Delivery Risk Intelligence  
**Data Science · Machine Learning · Data Engineering**

End-to-end analytical project focused on predicting delivery-delay risk using Brazilian e-commerce data.

Main themes:

- heterogeneous relational data;
- RAW → Bronze → Silver → Gold architecture;
- data contracts and analytical grain;
- Data Quality Gates;
- point-in-time feature governance;
- explicit prevention of data leakage;
- feature engineering;
- machine-learning experimentation;
- chronological and out-of-time validation.

The central engineering principle is simple: **only information genuinely available at prediction time should be allowed into the model**.

### CRUZEIRO

## Principles Behind the Work

Across different application domains, I try to preserve the same technical principles:

- **Mathematical rigor** before superficial complexity.
- **Reproducibility** instead of undocumented experimentation.
- **Temporal integrity** when building predictive systems.
- **Data quality and lineage** as part of the analytical system, not as an afterthought.
- **Validation of assumptions**, not only validation of metrics.
- **Interpretability and traceability** when models support decisions.
- **Clear distinction between implemented, simulated and proposed components.**
